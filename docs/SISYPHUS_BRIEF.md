# 《推石者：荒诞中的英雄》90-second production brief

Topic: Camus' absurd hero: awareness, revolt, and a freely chosen response despite futile external outcomes. Sisyphus is depicted as a giant-scale man as an artistic visual metaphor, not a historical/mythological claim.
Source: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/camus/

Specific art direction (NOT repo-wide): mythological engraved graphic-novel, layered granite, violet-black night moving toward warm dawn. Man physically touches giant boulder as pushing.

Seven narrative chapters:
0-12 establish uphill struggle;
12-24 pushes closer to summit;
24-35 rock escapes and crashes downhill; hands let go;
35-50 repeated effort and futility;
50-64 meaning-seeking meets world's silence;
64-78 conscious return; decision;
78-90 pushes again without falsely promising success.
