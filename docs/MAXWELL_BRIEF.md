# Maxwell's demon — 90-second production brief

Audience: general Mandarin-speaking viewers; mobile 9:16, 720×1280, 30 fps, 90 seconds. A new composition and `maxwell-demon` branch preserve all Sisyphus versions.

## Visual direction
An illustrated micro-laboratory: petrol night background, ivory glass boundaries, coral fast molecules, cyan slow molecules, amber observer/bit memory and mint controls. Rounded geometric demon with eyes and two tiny horns; no anatomical detail. Particle trails communicate actual speed. The same physical chamber persists while camera reframing reveals the controller, finite memory and heat bath. Keep scientific action above y=980; captions in x=48–672/y=1030–1170. No gratuitous cuts or decorative particle motion: gas motion is the explanatory subject.

## Scientific boundaries
The chamber contains identical-mass gas molecules. Colour identifies relative speed, not chemical species. Left/right warmth follows actual average squared speed in the illustrative model; no invented Kelvin values. Particle motion is an idealized noninteracting-gas visualization, not a molecular-dynamics experiment or an entropy measurement.

Sorting uses an ideal observer and door. Gas-only entropy reduction is the apparent paradox. A finite cyclic controller needs reusable memory. Show measurement and recording before reset. Measurement is not assigned an unavoidable kBT ln2 charge. For a standard unbiased one-bit memory in contact with a bath at T, isothermal reset of either unknown state to a fixed state has a minimum *average* dissipated heat kBT ln2 in the ideal reversible limit. State assumptions on screen if the bound is shown. Full-cycle accounting includes gas, controller/memory, environment and work sources; the total average entropy production is nonnegative. A fixed side temperature difference alone is not a full thermodynamic entropy computation.

Ending: information processing is physical, with applications to statistical physics and quantum measurement/feedback. Do not invoke uncertainty as the reason the classical demon fails or depict information as magically converted into energy without a heat source and controller accounting.

## Hard-shot gate and acceptance

Crossing is possible only inside the *actual open aperture*; a wrong-category candidate is reflected. Other particles may never be granted category-dependent ghost passage through a closed door. Preserve speed and kinetic energy on reflection and crossing. Check every substep crossing, wall bounds, particle count/identity and aperture clearance. Ensure several visible sorting events in the 10-second prototype and increasing mean kinetic energy on right relative to left.
