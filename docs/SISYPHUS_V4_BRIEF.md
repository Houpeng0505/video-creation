# Sisyphus V4 production brief

90 seconds, Mandarin, 9:16, 720×1280 / 30 fps. Preserve the V3 narration, score, cue boundaries and philosophical thesis. V3 source and released video remain unchanged. Work in branch `sisyphus-v4` with a separate composition and output.

## Direction
An illustrated night landscape: indigo, warm limestone, copper and turquoise. A readable jointed silhouette, a single marked round boulder, cut-paper mountain layers and restrained engraved contours. Detail supports weight and spatial relationships. Avoid anatomical embellishment. The ending leaves the summit unresolved.

Keep the art inside y=160–960 and captions inside x=56–664, y=1000–1150. Main titles use short lines above the action. Editorial cuts distinguish myth, philosophical explanation and deliberate action. The first ascent/release uses one continuous world with camera framing derived from that world.
